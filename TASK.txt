THE GAME — "ORBITAL SALVAGE" (arcade/score-chase, original concept):
You pilot a small salvage tug in a debris field orbiting a planet. Physics-based: your ship drifts, you thrust and rotate with keyboard (arrows/WASD), mouse aims the tractor beam. Debris pieces (rocks, broken satellites, glowing cargo pods) drift and collide. Use the tractor beam (hold left mouse) to reel cargo pods into your hold before they burn up in the planet's atmosphere below. Bigger pieces give more points but pull harder. Watch your hull: fast rocks damage you on impact. Fuel is limited — refill by docking at the orbiting station that circles the planet. Score = value salvaged; run ends when hull or fuel hits zero. Difficulty ramps: more junk, faster rocks, station orbit shrinks. Include: title screen with START, HUD (score, hull, fuel, hold capacity), a game-over screen with final score + RESTART, and at least 3 sound effects (WebAudio, generated, no files needed). Canvas 2D rendering, 60fps, smooth controls with inertia. Make it FEEL good: particles on capture, screen shake on impacts, glow effects.
